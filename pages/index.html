A better Hacker News

Dark mode, faster loading, favicons, archive links and a few other things. The script and styles that load on HN come to about 30 KB, or 13 KB compressed. Your login and votes still work.

One-click install for Firefox Download for Chrome Source on GitHub

What you get

  • Light, dark and system themes in HN's own colors, with the same layout
  • New comments marked when you reopen a story
  • Thread tools, ignore users, and blocklists for domains and keywords
  • Copy, archive.is and Wayback links on every story
  • Keyboard shortcuts: j and k to move, o for comments, l for the article

Install on Firefox

  • Click the button above.
  • __INSTALL_NOTE__
  • To try an unsigned build, open about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox, choose "Load Temporary Add-on", and pick manifest.json from the unzipped folder.

Install on Chrome

  • Unzip the download, open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, and choose "Load unpacked".
